Privacy policy for TradeTab

Last updated: 13 October 2026

TradeTab is a Shopify app that lets merchants offer house accounts: customers buy on account at the point of sale and pay later. To do that, the app stores a limited amount of data on behalf of the merchants who install it. This policy explains what we store, why, where it lives, and how it's deleted.

Information we collect

When a merchant installs TradeTab and opens house accounts, we store:

  • Store information — the store's domain and the API credentials Shopify issues so the app can operate on the store.
  • Customer information — for customers the merchant opens a house account for: their name, their Shopify customer ID, and an email address for account correspondence. We hold this only for customers with house accounts, not the store's whole customer base.
  • Transaction records — a ledger of account activity: charges, payments, refunds and adjustments, with amounts, due dates, references to the related Shopify orders, an optional purchase order number or note, and the staff member and store location associated with each charge.

We do not collect payment card details, passwords, or any data from store visitors or customers without a house account.

How we use it

The data above is used solely to provide TradeTab's features: tracking account balances, enforcing credit limits, creating orders with payment terms, printing receipts, and showing account activity to the merchant. We do not sell data, use it for advertising or marketing, or share it with third parties other than the hosting providers below.

Where it's stored

The app runs on Fly.io (hosted in London, UK) and its database is hosted by Supabase in the EU. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). TradeTab processes this data on behalf of the merchant, who remains the controller of their customers' data.

Retention and deletion

  • Customer redaction — when a customer asks a merchant to erase their data, Shopify notifies TradeTab and we automatically anonymise the customer's house account: the name is replaced, and the email address and notes are removed. Transaction amounts are retained as the merchant's financial records, no longer linked to any personal details.
  • Data requests — when a customer asks for a copy of their data, Shopify notifies TradeTab and we support the merchant in compiling the export.
  • Uninstalling — when a merchant uninstalls TradeTab, Shopify sends a deletion request and all accounts, ledgers and statements we hold for that store are permanently deleted.
  • Merchants can also delete individual house accounts in the app at any time.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page and take effect when posted.
